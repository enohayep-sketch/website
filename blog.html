AI Fixed & Stability Improvements

Great news! We have successfully fixed the issues with our AI systems by officially switching to OpenRouter. Stability is back up!

Development Update & Potential Delay

Hello everyone! Today we have an update regarding EazySploit's development status.

First off, the official release might be slightly delayed. We are currently waiting for ApolloAI to come back online, as their downtime has caused our AI-powered features to break. We aren't entirely sure of ApolloAI's current status, so we are also considering a switch to OpenRouter to ensure stability. Additionally, the integrated chat system and the login menu's video player are experiencing some minor issues at the moment.

On the bright side, the rest of the executor is looking pretty gucci! We still have some polishing to do with the UI elements and we need to address a crash issue related to startup scripts. We'll be working hard on these fixes tomorrow.

Stay tuned for more updates!
