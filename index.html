About

EazySploit is a free Roblox executor with extensive features. It's free because it uses the public Quorum Velocity API.

Kept private due to the extensive features and development time - about 4-5 months of rewritten development. Originally created in 2021 using AxonDLL.

Main developers: Rago (Eazy/Dog-Ware, previous owner of Evil X/Evil X Reborn, previously staff at HectaSploit) and F34R (previous owner of seryiu, Fear X). HectaSploit by lil french reaper and NoobHaxx were major inspirations, HectaSploit being the exploit with the most APIs.
